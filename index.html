FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know About Cash for Gold in Chennai

Clear answers regarding testing methodology, payment safety, and required documents.

What documents are required to sell gold in Chennai?

To ensure 100% legal compliance and security, please bring any valid government ID (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Passport) along with address verification. Original purchase bills are welcome if available, but not strictly mandatory.

How is the purity of my gold tested?

We use state-of-the-art German X-ray fluorescence (XRF) spectroscopy analyzers. This laboratory-grade technique determines the exact karatage and elemental composition down to 0.01% accuracy without scratching, melting, or damaging your jewelry.

How does releasing pledged gold work?

Simply bring your pledge loan ticket/receipt. Our representative will visit the bank or pawn shop with you, pay off the full outstanding debt directly, retrieve your gold, evaluate it, and disburse the remaining market surplus balance to you immediately.

What payment modes are offered for my gold?

We provide instant spot cash (subject to statutory RBI limits) or instant electronic bank settlement via RTGS, NEFT, or IMPS directly into your verified bank account before you leave our branch.
