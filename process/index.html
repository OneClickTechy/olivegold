Frequently Asked Questions About Our Valuation

Clear answers to how we evaluate, protect, and value your precious assets.

Can I take my jewellery back if I decide not to sell after the valuation?

Absolutely 100% yes. Because our German XRF testing is entirely non-destructive and we never melt your ornaments, your jewellery remains completely untouched and intact. Our valuation is completely free with zero obligation. If you choose not to proceed, you simply take your ornaments home with our complimentary valuation sheet.

Do you charge any testing fee or consultation charges?

No. The ultrasonic cleaning, digital weighing, German XRF laser purity analysis, and market valuation estimate are provided completely free of charge as part of our transparent client service.

What if I do not have the original purchase invoice or receipt?

Many families inherit ancestral gold or misplace decades-old jewellery bills. You can still sell your gold without the original invoice. All you need is a valid government photo ID (Aadhaar, Passport, or Voter ID) and sign a simple standard ownership declaration on-site.

How long does the entire 4-step process take from start to finish?

For typical family jewellery collections (1 to 20 ornaments), the complete cycle of cleaning, weighing, German XRF testing, price calculation, and bank transfer takes only 10 to 15 minutes. You can enjoy refreshments in our air-conditioned VIP lounge while our staff handles the documentation.

Can you release pledged gold from banks or pawn brokers?

Yes! We specialize in releasing pledged gold from banks, NBFCs, and pawn brokers across Chennai. We provide the initial funds to settle the loan balance, safely retrieve your gold ornaments from the bank vault, re-evaluate the purity with German XRF, and transfer the remaining cash balance directly to you.
