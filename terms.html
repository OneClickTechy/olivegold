PREAMBLE

Customer Agreement & Operating Guidelines

Welcome to Olive Gold Company. By initiating any appraisal, valuation, or sale of precious metals at any of our branches or via doorstep concierge, you acknowledge and accept the terms set forth below. These terms safeguard both parties and ensure complete alignment with the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and the Government of India.

1. Age & Eligibility Criteria

To execute a sale of precious metals, the customer must be at least 18 years of age and possess full legal capacity to enter into binding commercial contracts. A valid original government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Passport, PAN Card, or Voter ID) along with local address verification is mandatory.

2. Legal Ownership Warranty

The customer warrants and guarantees that they are the sole, lawful owner of all gold, silver, or diamonds presented, or are duly authorized in writing by the lawful owner. Olive Gold operates under a zero-tolerance policy regarding encumbered, stolen, or disputed assets and reports any suspicious activity to local law enforcement.

3. Non-Destructive Scientific Valuation

All precious metals are tested in the direct physical presence of the customer using calibrated electronic X-ray fluorescence (XRF) spectroscopy analyzers and certified precision micro-balances (0.001g). The customer retains the complete right to decline the quotation and retrieve their items unaltered at zero cost.

4. Live Pricing & Instant Disbursement

Quoted valuations are linked directly to real-time bullion market indices at the timestamp of calculation. Upon written customer consent, disbursement is processed via spot cash (within statutory limits) or electronic banking channels (IMPS / RTGS / NEFT) to a verified account bearing the customer’s name.

5. Finality of Transaction

Once the customer signs the formal computer invoice and disbursement has been released, the purchase is deemed legally concluded, irreversible, and non-refundable. Re-purchase of the same items is not feasible once bullion is routed into treasury refining.

6. Confidentiality & Data Protection

All customer transaction records, identification details, and valuation records are strictly archived in encrypted, secure servers in accordance with the Information Technology Act and Indian Privacy Laws. We do not sell or disclose customer information to third-party commercial marketers.
