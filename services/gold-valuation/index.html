Why Scientific XRF Assay Beats Traditional Acid Testing

For decades, local pawn brokers and gold buyers scraped jewelry across black touchstones and applied corrosive nitric acid. This archaic method permanently damages heirloom ornaments and relies on subjective visual estimation that often underestimates karatage.

At Olive Gold, our German XRF spectrometry stations utilize high-energy X-ray fluorescence to instantly analyze atomic emission spectra. Within 45 seconds, our computer screen displays the exact percentage of pure gold (Au), silver (Ag), copper (Cu), and zinc (Zn) with absolute scientific certainty.
